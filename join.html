Artist benefits

  • Free to join and to upload
  • You keep ownership of your tracks and artwork
  • An artist dashboard to manage your uploads
  • Choose Public, Unlisted or Private for every track
  • Choose whether listeners can download your music
  • Mark explicit content so listeners know what to expect
  • Upload music videos as well as audio

Artist requirements

  • Music you own or have permission to distribute
  • Audio in MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, OGG or AIFF
  • A track title and your artist name
  • Artwork in JPG or PNG, at least 1400×1400 pixels
  • A confirmation that your upload does not infringe anyone else's copyright

Respecting other artists' rights keeps YAPSMusic fair for everyone. Content reported for copyright is reviewed.

Questions artists ask

Is it really free to join?

Yes. Signing up as an artist on YAPSMusic is free.

Who owns my music?

You do. You keep ownership of your tracks and artwork. By uploading, you give YAPSMusic a licence to host, stream and display them on the platform.

What file types can I upload?

The upload screen accepts MP3, WAV, FLAC, AAC, OGG and AIFF audio, plus video files. Artwork should be a JPG or PNG, at least 1400×1400 pixels.

Can I upload a song I did not make?

No. Only upload music you own or have permission to distribute. You confirm this before every release is published.

Can I choose who hears my track?

Yes. Set each track to Public, Unlisted (only people with the link) or Private (only you), and choose whether listeners can download it.

Can I mark explicit content?

Yes. There is an explicit-content option on every upload.

What if someone uploads my music without permission?

Use the copyright reporting option in the app and give enough detail for the content to be identified and reviewed.

I have a question that is not answered here.

Open the Help Center inside the app, or send feedback from the app menu. We read what you send to improve YAPSMusic.
