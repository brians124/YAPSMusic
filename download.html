Two connected experiences

The website You are here

A home for YAPSMusic: trending songs, new releases, featured artists, and information about the platform. You can sample a track here.

The app Separate

The full YAPSMusic experience, with search, your library, playlists, following artists, videos and uploads for artists. Every song and artist link on the website leads into it.

Website for discovery, app for everything you do next.

What you can do in the app

Listen to music

Stream songs from Uganda, East Africa and beyond, with a mini-player that keeps going while you browse.

Discover artists

Search, browse genres, check charts and new releases to find your next favourite sound.

Watch videos

Music videos live in the app alongside the songs, so you can switch between listening and watching.

Follow artists

Open an artist profile and follow them to keep up with their music.

Create playlists

Build playlists, like songs and keep your library in one place.

Become an artist

Upload your own tracks for free from the app and share them with listeners.
