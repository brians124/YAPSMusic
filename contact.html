Frequently asked questions

How do I get help with the app?

Open the YAPSMusic app and use the Help Center. If something is broken, choose Report a Problem and describe what happened, what you were doing and, if relevant, the song or artist involved.

How can an artist get their music on YAPSMusic?

Artists upload from inside the app, and joining is free. The Become an Artist page explains the steps and requirements.

I found my music on YAPSMusic and I did not upload it.

Use the copyright reporting option in the app and give enough detail for the content to be identified and reviewed. Only music that the uploader has the rights to distribute should be on YAPSMusic.

Something on an artist profile is wrong.

Report the incorrect information through Report a Problem or the artist support topic in the form above.

Can I suggest a feature or a song or artist to add?

Yes. Send feedback from the app menu, or choose General inquiry in the form above.

How do I ask about advertising or a partnership?

Choose Advertising / sponsorship or Partnership inquiry in the form and tell us about your brand or organisation, what you have in mind and any timing.

Is the website the same as the app?

No. The website is where you discover YAPSMusic and the app is where you listen, build playlists and follow artists. See Get the app.
