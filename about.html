What is YAPSMusic?

A place to discover, listen to, organise and share music from Uganda, Africa and around the world.

YAPSMusic brings songs, artists, videos and playlists together in one home, available as a website and as an installable app. It is made in Kampala, with Ugandan music at its heart: Afrobeats, Afropop, Luganda Pop, Dancehall, R&B and Gospel.

Our mission

To take Ugandan music to listeners everywhere, by making it easy for artists to share their work and for people to find it, play it and pass it on.

Our vision

A world where music from Uganda sits naturally beside the music people already love, and where every artist has a fair way to be discovered.

“Music to the World, from Uganda”

This is more than a tagline. It is where YAPSMusic starts: the sound of Kampala and East Africa, in the languages and rhythms its artists grew up with, sent out to anyone willing to listen.

What we do

Supporting artists

Artists can upload their tracks, build a profile and follow how their music is doing from an artist dashboard. Music is uploaded only when the artist has the rights to share it.

Connecting artists with listeners

Listeners can follow artists, like songs, build playlists and watch music videos, so a new release can reach the people who care about it.

Global music discovery

Charts, new releases, genres, search and a built-in AI playlist builder help people anywhere find something new from Uganda and beyond.
